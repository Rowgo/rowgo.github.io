Project Overveiw

King of snowball mountain is a online multiplayer third person shooter where you and your friends play as ice spirits having a snowball fight. The way you win is staying is just like king of the mountain, stay on the preak the longest, but instead of pushing you your friends will be trying to knock you off using exploding snowballs.

I wanted to learn how to make online multiplayer games using unreal engine so I started taking a course on Udemy on how to make an online multiplayer shooter. Eventually I branched off of the course so I could apply the concepts into an original project to better help me learn. Which is how this game came about, from trying to think of a project that could use both the art from the course and the mechanics.

If you want to see how the game is coming along then you can check out my twitch channel RowgaBear.
