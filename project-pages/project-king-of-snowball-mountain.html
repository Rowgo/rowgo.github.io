Project Overveiw

King of snowball mountain is an online multiplayer, third person shooter, where you and your friends play as ice spirits. The way you win is by staying staying on the peak the longest while your friends try to knock you off the top using exploding snowballs.

This game came about when I branched the project off from the course I'm taking on how to make online multiplayer games, so that I could apply the concpets in the course and enhance my learning.

If you want to see how the game is coming along then you can check out my twitch channel RowgaBear.
