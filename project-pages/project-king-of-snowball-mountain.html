Project Overview

King of snowball mountain is an online multiplayer, third person shooter, where you and your friends play as ice spirits. Try to stay on the peak while your friends throw exploding snowballs at you, if you stay on the peak the longest, you win.

This game came about when I branched off of the project from the course I'm taking on online multiplayer games in Unreal Engine. I branched off the course so that I could apply the concepts in new ways and enhance my learning.

If you want to see how the game is coming along then you can check out my twitch channel RowgaBear.
