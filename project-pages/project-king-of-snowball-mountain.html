Project Overveiw

King of snowball mountain is a online multiplayer third person shooter where you and your friends play as ice spirits having a snowball fight. The way you win is staying on top of the mountain long enough to capture the point, but while you do so your friends will be trying to knock you off using exploding snowballs.

I wanted to learn how to make online multiplayer games using unreal engine so I started taking a course on Udemy on how to make a online multiplayer shooter. Eventually I got far enough in that I wanted to stretch out and see what I could do on my own, so I came up with the idea for King Of Snowball Mountain after imagining what kind of game or world the character we were using for the project would live in.

If you want to see how the game is coming along then you can check out my twitch channel RowgaBear.
